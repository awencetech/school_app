Privacy Policy for MMHS

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MMHS ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the MMHS school application.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, MMHS may process information such as:

  • Name and school-related identification information.
  • Contact information such as phone number or email address, when provided for account or communication purposes.
  • School information such as class, attendance, academic information, notices, or other information required to provide school services.
  • Information you voluntarily submit through the app.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the school's app features.
  • Authenticate users and manage access.
  • Display school-related information and communications.
  • Maintain app security and prevent misuse.
  • Fix technical problems and improve reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to provide the app and school services, comply with law, protect users or the service, or when authorized by the school or the user.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app and school services, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable legal requirements. Retention periods may depend on the type of information and the school's requirements.

6. Children's Privacy

MMHS is a school-related application and may be used by students. Where student information is processed, it is handled for legitimate school and educational purposes and in accordance with applicable laws and school requirements. We do not knowingly use children's personal information for targeted advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for functions such as hosting, authentication, notifications, analytics, crash reporting, maps, or other technical services. If such services are used, their processing of information is governed by their applicable privacy policies.

8. Permissions

The app may request device permissions only when a feature requires them. For example, camera, storage, notifications, location, or other permissions may be requested if those features are implemented. You can manage available permissions through your device settings.

9. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may contact the school/app administrator to ask about the personal information associated with your use of the app, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where applicable and legally permitted.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published at this location with a revised "Last updated" date.
